A parsed observation flows through every stage. Each judges independently; the pipeline folds the verdicts and keeps the worst. The rings above are these stages, outer to inner.

Held to a
hard bar.

Every primitive carries property tests that assert the laws it relies on across the whole input space, a cargo-mutants sweep that leaves zero survivors, and documentation on every public item that opens with a worked example — run as a doctest.

Eleven mitigation primitives plus the deflect-kit umbrella. Defensive by construction: every stage decides what to do with traffic arriving at infrastructure the operator controls.
